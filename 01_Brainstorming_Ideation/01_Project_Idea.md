# EduGenie – Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title
EduGenie – AI-Powered Learning Assistant

## Problem Statement
Students may face difficulties in understanding academic topics and getting immediate learning support.

## Proposed Solution
EduGenie is an AI-powered learning assistant that helps students understand academic topics through simple explanations and examples.

## Main Objective
The main objective of EduGenie is to provide students with an easy-to-use AI-based learning platform.

## Target Users
- School Students
- College Students
- Learners

## Main Features
- AI-based question answering
- Simple topic explanations
- Example-based learning
- Interactive learning support
- User-friendly interface

## Expected Outcome
EduGenie will help students understand academic concepts easily and provide quick learning assistance.
